# Job Application Engine

## Load the right evidence

1. Read the candidate's private fact sheet before assessing fit or drafting claims.
2. Read `references/voice-guide.md` before writing first-person material.
3. Read the task-specific reference:
   - discovery or fit: `references/job-discovery-and-fit.md`
   - resume: `references/resume-tailoring.md`
   - cover letter or screening response: `references/cover-letter-strategy.md`
   - SEO take-home: `references/seo-assessment-framework.md`
4. Read `references/privacy-and-claim-safety.md` before creating public artifacts or transmitting application data.
5. Use the candidate's current editable resume as the authoritative source for chronology and wording. Treat older PDFs as visual references only.

## Run the workflow

1. Inspect the live posting and distinguish requirements from employer branding.
2. Verify title, employer, location, compensation, posting date, closing date, and preferred application route when available.
3. Grade fit before rewriting anything. Identify strong evidence, adjacent experience, real gaps, and likely interview probes.
4. Build a compact evidence map from employer need to verified proof.
5. Tailor only where relevance or clarity improves. Preserve chronology, factual scope, and readable layout.
6. Draft any cover letter or screening response in the candidate's actual voice, not a generic professional voice.
7. Cross-check every artifact for employer, role, dates, titles, metrics, scope, and terminology.
8. Require human review before uploading personal files or entering sensitive data.
9. Require a final, action-time human approval before submitting an application.
10. Record the application and monitor only the approved communication channels.

## Non-negotiable guardrails

- Never invent experience, metrics, dates, credentials, clients, or tools.
- Preserve qualifiers that keep a claim honest: `approximately`, `attributed`, measurement source, window, and team contribution.
- Do not convert human-in-the-loop systems into claims of full autonomy.
- Do not expose candidate-private files in public repositories or examples.
- Do not optimize for keyword overlap at the expense of readability or truth.
- Do not submit, send, or publish without the appropriate human approval.
- Explain meaningful gaps rather than hiding them with vague prose.

## Output standard

Lead with the recommendation. Separate must-change items from optional refinements. Use the resume for proof, the cover letter for interpretation and judgment, screening answers for direct evidence, and interview preparation for claim defense.
