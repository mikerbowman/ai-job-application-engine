# Candidate Voice Guide

## Aim

Preserve a recognizable human voice while improving clarity. The result should sound experienced, candid, curious, and intellectually alive, not like corporate wallpaper.

## Preferred qualities

- Lead with the point and vary sentence length.
- Use concrete language, defensible claims, and business consequences.
- Allow a little edge: call a low-impact item a nitpick; question black-box claims; distinguish strategy from checklist hygiene.
- Connect curiosity to testing: question assumptions, including your own; measure; share; iterate; abandon what does not work.
- Use precise technical language when it proves competence, then connect it to a human or business outcome.
- Preserve one or two flashes of imperfect humanity in longer prose when appropriate: a cheeky phrase, an aside, or industry shorthand.
- Prefer natural contractions and direct constructions.
- Use exclamation marks sparingly and only when enthusiasm earns one.

## Cadence

- Prefer commas and conjunctions over a parade of polished em-dash pivots.
- Mildly additive or run-on cadence can be a feature when it remains readable.
- Adjacent, nearly synonymous words are acceptable when the second adds real meaning or direction.
- Use `aim` for a flexible intended outcome, `goal` for a firmer target, and `work` for the action that moves toward either.

## Avoid

- “Thrilled to apply,” “results-driven professional,” and similar canned openings.
- Perfectly symmetrical paragraphs and three-item slogans.
- Punchy AI conclusions such as “That distinction matters” when the distinction should be explained.
- Empty superlatives, tool clouds, or keyword stuffing.
- Blame, bitterness, gossip, or inside-baseball drama.
- Sanding away every unusual phrase until the candidate sounds interchangeable.

## Calibration

- Resume: compressed and factual.
- Cover letter: professional, but with a visible human behind it.
- Screening answer: direct answer, reasoning, evidence.
- SEO assessment: decisive, prioritized, mildly opinionated.
- LinkedIn: searchable and conversational.
