# Job Discovery and Fit

## Discovery

- Search title families rather than one exact title.
- Apply the candidate's location, compensation, schedule, and employment-type constraints.
- Prefer recent listings and capture posting age and explicit closing dates.
- Verify the live employer or ATS page rather than trusting an aggregator snippet.
- Prefer application routes in this order unless the candidate says otherwise: employer site, official ATS, third-party recruiter, professional network, broad aggregator.
- Deduplicate on canonical URL plus employer, title, and location.

## Fit score

Score the role against:

- central business outcome;
- required capabilities;
- strength and recency of evidence;
- location and compensation;
- preferred or differentiating capabilities.

A high keyword match with weak outcome evidence is not a high fit. A candidate may still be a strong fit with an adjacent industry background when the operating discipline and outcomes transfer.

## Report

For every credible prospect, capture:

- employer and title;
- location and working model;
- compensation and whether it is verified;
- posting age and closing date;
- preferred application URL and route;
- fit score and grade;
- strongest evidence;
- meaningful gaps or likely probes;
- tailoring emphasis;
- concise reason to apply or skip.
