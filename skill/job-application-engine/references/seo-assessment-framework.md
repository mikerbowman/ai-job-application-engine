# SEO and AEO Assessment Framework

## Lead with impact

Organize recommendations by business and search impact, not crawl order.

### Strategic improvements

- Search intent and information gain
- Differentiation and proof
- Conversion evidence and calls to action
- Content architecture and internal discovery
- Relevant authority and link gaps
- Measurement tied to qualified visibility and business outcomes

### Hygiene and maintainability

Then address metadata, canonicals, indexability, structured data, robots controls, sitemaps, headings, image delivery, mobile UX, Core Web Vitals, broken links, template maintainability, and accessibility.

Label low-impact recommendations honestly. A technically valid observation is not automatically a strategic priority.

## Claim discipline

- Do not keyword-stuff alt text; decorative images may need empty alt text.
- Do not call an image the LCP element without verification.
- Do not present headings, filenames, or modern image formats as major ranking levers.
- Distinguish backlinks from referring domains.
- Treat `site:` counts and AI-assisted crawl classifications as leads, not definitive audits.
- Identify the source and limitations of AI-visibility measurements.

## Response shape

1. Diagnose the page's job and biggest constraint.
2. Give three to five high-impact recommendations with rationale.
3. Show evidence supporting prioritization.
4. Separate hygiene findings.
5. Explain what to validate next with first-party analytics, crawling, competitive research, backlink analysis, and conversion data.
