# Privacy and Claim Safety

## Keep private

- resumes and cover letters containing contact information;
- prospect trackers and application-status logs;
- employer-specific application packets;
- email correspondence and recruiter names;
- compensation answers and legal attestations;
- proprietary dashboards, screenshots, exports, and customer data;
- private professional stories containing names or internal politics;
- credentials, API keys, tokens, cookies, and connector configuration.

## Safe public examples

- synthetic job descriptions;
- clearly labeled placeholder metrics;
- generic candidate fact templates;
- redacted or fictionalized workflow demonstrations;
- process documentation and evaluation rubrics.

## Claim standard

Every prominent claim should have:

1. a baseline and measurement window;
2. the candidate's role versus the team's role;
3. actions and decision logic;
4. a source or measurement method;
5. the result, caveats, and what the candidate learned.

If one of those is missing, qualify the claim or omit it.
