# Application Workflow

## Preparation

1. Reopen the live listing and confirm it is still available.
2. Inventory every required and optional field before entering data.
3. Prepare the tailored resume, cover letter, and direct answers.
4. Show the candidate any unexpected question before answering it.
5. Confirm compensation, work authorization, availability, and demographic-field preferences rather than guessing.

## Quality control

- Use the same evidence and numbers across every artifact.
- Check that uploaded filenames are professional and employer-specific.
- Treat optional cover letters as part of the package when they add value.
- Leave voluntary demographic fields unanswered unless the candidate directs otherwise.
- Do not silently improvise start dates, salary demands, sponsorship status, or legal attestations.

## Approval gates

Human approval is required before:

- transmitting contact information or personal files;
- answering an unforeseen substantive question;
- solving or handing off a verification challenge;
- submitting the final application.

After submission, verify an explicit confirmation state and log the result.
