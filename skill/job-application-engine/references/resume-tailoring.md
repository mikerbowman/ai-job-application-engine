# Resume Tailoring

## Assess before editing

Map the role into the relevant capability buckets. Mark each as strong, adjacent, or a real gap. Recommend applying when the candidate can credibly deliver the central outcome and meets most must-haves, even if some preferred qualifications are missing.

## Tailor selectively

- Preserve employers, chronology, titles, and factual scope.
- Reorder bullets to surface the most relevant proof.
- Mirror employer terminology only when it accurately describes prior work.
- Prefer one meaningful scale marker and one outcome over a wall of numbers.
- Use outcome language where evidence exists; ownership itself can also be relevant proof.
- Keep recent experience fullest and compress older roles.
- Improve an existing bullet before adding a redundant keyword bullet.

## Metrics

- Use exact-looking numbers only when the candidate can explain the source and window.
- Use honest approximations for ballpark figures.
- Preserve qualifiers such as measurement source, `attributed`, or `over four weeks` when they prevent overclaiming.
- For shared outcomes, use language such as `helped scale`, `contributed to`, or `led the work supporting`.

## Layout

Default to two pages for an experienced candidate unless the situation warrants otherwise. Protect readable type, margins, spacing, section hierarchy, and ATS-safe text before adding material.

## Final check

Verify every changed claim against the candidate fact sheet, then compare the resume with the cover letter and screening answers for contradictions.
